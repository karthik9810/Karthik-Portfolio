About me

I’m Karthik, a creative designer working across AI image generation, graphic design, and visual branding. I turn ideas into purposeful visuals—from logos and mascots to social media designs, thumbnails, and polished product images.

My skill set combines prompt engineering with Canva and Adobe Photoshop. I bring together concept development, composition, typography, and image editing to create a consistent visual direction for each project.

Whether I’m shaping a brand identity or creating a single image, my focus is clear: understand the idea, explore the possibilities, and refine the details into a design that communicates.
